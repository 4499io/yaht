## Description (max 4000)

yaht is yet another habit tracker — but a quiet one.

Track anything you want to do more of: a daily workout, your prescriptions, calling your mum on the weekend. Set the schedule that fits the habit, pick an emoji and a color, and let your days quietly fill in.

Built for the evening, not the notification. yaht is always dark and deliberately calm — no bright screens in a dim room, no confetti, no guilt when you miss a day.

**Track it your way**
• Yes/No habits for the things you either did or didn't
• Count habits for the things you tally — eight glasses of water, thirty pushups
• Schedules that match real life: every day, specific weekdays, every few days, or a few times a week

**See your progress at a glance**
• A GitHub-style activity grid for every habit, in its own color
• One global grid that blends all your habits together — the more you do, the richer the day

**Reminders that respect you**
• Set your own times, with different schedules for weekdays and weekends
• Choose a custom sound per habit
• That's it — no nagging, no streak-shaming

**Yours, everywhere, privately**
• Your habits and history sync across your iPhone with iCloud
• Nothing is collected, tracked, or sent to us — your data stays yours

Fake it until you make it. Build the habit, internalize it, and let the grid remind you how far you've come.

yaht is built exclusively for iPhone.

## Privacy (App Privacy "nutrition label")
**Data Not Collected.** yaht has no analytics, no accounts, no third-party SDKs. Habit data lives
on-device (SwiftData) and syncs through the user's **private iCloud/CloudKit database** — Apple's
infrastructure, not ours; this is *not* "data collected by the developer." Declare every category as
**Not Collected** in App Store Connect → App Privacy.

A privacy policy URL is still required. Minimal true statement: "yaht does not collect, store, or
transmit any personal data to its developer. Your habits are stored on your device and, if you enable
iCloud, in your own private iCloud account."
